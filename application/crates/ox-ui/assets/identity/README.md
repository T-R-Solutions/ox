# Embedded Ox identity

Supplied by the maintainer in `.local/assets/definitivo/`, identity revision
`identidade-definitiva-01`, mascot revision `algodao-etiqueta-vetorial-05`.
Selected combination: Catalisador v2 (study 06) symbol, Valência v1 (study 01)
lettering/composition, cotton mascot (v5 study 17) with the approved Ferris tag.

These three PNGs remain byte-identical source copies. The two signatures are
embedded at compile time; the mascot is retained as historical artwork and is
not loaded or rendered by the UI after the 2026-11-17 request. Neither
`.local/assets` nor an image generator is needed to build/run the app. Native
Iced images retain the original aspect ratio and alpha. The signature is 960×300;
the mascot is 1065×1477. The navy identity region uses the light-on-dark signature;
High Contrast uses the supplied all-white signature. Reserve at least 14/320 of
the displayed signature width externally. No tint, crop or mascot modification.

Brand RGB: navy `#243C59`, copper `#B96F46`; dark signature soft white `#F6FAFE`
and light copper `#E4B695`. These are identity colors, not action/status roles.
The signature lettering is original vector geometry, not a new font.

Provenance follows the maintainer's definitive README. Ferris attribution:
Karen Rustad Tölva, [Rustacean.net](https://rustacean.net/); that source declares
a waiver of copyright and related rights to the extent permitted by law.
The retained mascot is raster, including its small tag; the upstream composition
has a vector tag over a raster body. No claim of full mascot vectorization,
animation, new external license audit or trademark registration.
