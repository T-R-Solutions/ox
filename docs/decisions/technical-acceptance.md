# ADR — Evidence-based technical skill acceptance

Adopted under the maintainer request of 2026-11-01. This decision supersedes only the unconditional requirement that the nine skills AC-01..04 remain UNVERIFIED in product-foundation AC-03 and the preparatory implementation-transition decision. Those original records remain historical and byte-preserved; their other obligations and all 36 scenario definitions remain intact. Current policy is governed by this ADR and sdd/features/technical-acceptance.

Require schema 2, criterion-specific semantic review and concrete consumer/oracle execution for technical APPROVED/REJECTED. INCONCLUSIVE requires a bound review explaining the insufficient observation; NOT APPLICABLE requires a bound review and explicit scope justification. UNVERIFIED needs no fictitious execution. Normalize legacy status names. This is evidence validation, not an authorization mechanism or proof of authenticity.

A technical_review version 1 object is attached to the requirement. scope and review are nonempty strings; execution is a list of evidence IDs. APPROVED/REJECTED additionally require consumer and oracle paths. The review must be a linked inspection, with bound spec/plan/tasks/traceability and the traced skill/oracle. For decided criteria it must also bind the declared concrete consumer/oracle. Every declared execution must be linked test/trace/benchmark evidence, bind both concrete inputs with implementation/oracle roles, and refer to a different artifact than the review. No borrowing an unrelated evidence ID or passing authoring inspection as execution.

Portable skill acceptance may target frozen copies of actually observed consumer/test sources. Explicit source identities, snapshot hashes, method and limitations must accompany the review. The consumer need not be the current Ox runtime. This does not confer current product acceptance or promise that a future source still matches. Product features retain independent evidence. Checking hashes and structure cannot determine whether a log is truthful, a test is independent or the complete dependencies were declared; semantic review remains mandatory.

Alternatives rejected: unconditional freeze suppresses legitimate rejection/inconclusive states; simply deleting the guard permits documentation-only promotion; importing a product verdict by AC ID conflates independent contracts. Revisit on evidence-schema, distribution-boundary or consumer-scope changes. No deployment, publication, sandbox adoption or product expansion follows.
